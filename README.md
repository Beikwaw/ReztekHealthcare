# Dr-Mkhonza-health-practice

## Simple Workflow Guide

This guide explains how the **Clerk** and **Doctor** use the system day to day.

### Clerk Workflow
1. **Register a new patient**
   - Go to `Register Patient`.
   - Enter patient details. The **date of birth auto-fills from the ID number**.
   - Save the patient (they are added to the waiting list).

2. **Search or update a patient**
   - Go to `Search Patients`.
   - Open a patient to view details or edit their profile.

3. **Send patient to the doctor**
   - From any patient list, click **Send to Dr**.
   - The patient appears in the doctor’s waiting list.

4. **Create invoices after consultation**
   - When a doctor completes a consultation, the patient shows as **Completed**.
   - Go to **Completed Invoices** in the sidebar.
   - Create an invoice for the patient.
   - Send the invoice by **Email** or **WhatsApp**.
   - Once sent, the invoice is marked **Completed** and moves to the Completed list.

### Doctor Workflow
1. **See waiting patients**
   - Go to `Waiting Patients`.
   - Start a consultation from the waiting list.

2. **Begin a consultation**
   - Open `Consultations` and start or continue a session.
   - Use the **checkbox-based symptom capture** form.
   - Generate the AI-assisted summary, review it, and confirm.

3. **Complete a consultation**
   - Click **Complete Consultation** once finished.
   - The patient status changes to **Completed** for the clerk to invoice.

### Notes
- The system works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Only staff use this system (patients do not log in).
